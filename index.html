    Backup & restore

    Your classes, rosters, and records are saved only in this browser on this computer. If you clear your browser data, switch browsers, or use another computer, they won't be there. Download a backup regularly and keep the file somewhere safe, like your Google Drive or a USB stick.

    Restoring replaces everything in the app with what's in the backup file.

    Spreadsheet export

    Save one class's records as a CSV file that opens in Excel or Google Sheets. Columns: Name, Date, Outcome.

    🔒 Private by design: student names never leave this computer. Nothing you type is uploaded — there's no server and no account. Everything is saved in this browser.
